# Privacy Policy — RentMatchAI

**Effective Date:** November 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 20, 2026  

Welcome to **RentMatchAI** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy, security, and personal data of our users across our platform at [rentmatchai.vercel.app](https://rentmatchai.vercel.app/).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and safeguard personal information collected from prospective tenants, property owners, and platform visitors.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Provided by Users
* **Account Credentials:** Name, email address, phone number, and password created during registration as a Tenant or Property Lister.
* **Tenant Search Preferences:** Budget constraints (maximum/minimum rent), preferred localities (e.g., Kudasan, Infocity, Sargasan, Raysan, Vavol), desired BHK type, furnishing preference, and commute parameters.
* **Landlord & Property Data:** Property address, monthly rent, security deposit terms, room specifications, carpet area, photographs, and ownership verification documents (e.g., electricity bills, tax receipts, or property deed proofs).
* **Direct Inquiries:** Contact information shared when scheduling property visits or initiating conversations with landlords via WhatsApp.

### B. Automatically Collected Information
* **Technical Data:** Browser type, device identifiers, IP address, and pages visited within the application.
* **Local Storage:** Client-side persistence for user interface state, such as dark/light theme mode and shortlisted favorite properties (`rentmatch-saved`).

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly to deliver and improve our rental matching services:
1. **AI-Assisted Compatibility Matching:** Analyzing tenant constraints against property attributes to calculate personalized compatibility scores.
2. **Deed Verification:** Authenticating property ownership to maintain a verified, broker-free platform.
3. **Communication Routing:** Enabling verified tenants to connect directly with property owners via WhatsApp without middleman fees.
4. **Platform Security & Reliability:** Preventing fraudulent listings, unauthorized scraping, spam, and technical malfunctions.

---

## 3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

* **No Data Selling:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party brokers, marketing agencies, or data aggregators.
* **Direct Landlord-Tenant Contact:** Contact numbers are only disclosed when a user clicks the "Contact / Enquire" button to initiate direct communication.
* **Infrastructure Providers:** We rely on secure third-party services (such as Vercel for web hosting and Google OAuth for authentication) solely to deliver core functionality under confidentiality obligations.
* **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose information if mandated by applicable Indian law, court order, or official law enforcement inquiry.

---

## 4. Data Security & Storage

We maintain appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal data against loss, theft, unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration. All web traffic between your browser and our servers is secured using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption.

---

## 5. User Rights & Data Control

Users possess the following rights regarding their data:
* **Access & Correction:** You can view and update your personal profile information and rental search preferences anytime under `/profile`.
* **Account Deletion:** You may request the deletion of your user account, search preferences, and active property listings by emailing support.
* **Shortlist Management:** Saved properties stored in your browser's local storage can be cleared directly from your browser settings at any time.

---

## 6. Age Restrictions

RentMatchAI is intended exclusively for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
